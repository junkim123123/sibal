6. FAQ SECTION
================================================================================

Question 1
What is the 49 dollar deposit for

Answer 1
The deposit reserves your dedicated sourcing expert and activates your project. It prioritizes factory quoting and ensures you receive 3 verified quotes within 7 business days. Your deposit is 100 percent credited to your first invoice when you place an order.

Question 2
Is the deposit refundable

Answer 2
Your deposit is applied as credit to your first invoice when you place an order. If you don't proceed with an order, deposit policies may vary by project. Contact our support team to discuss your specific situation.

Question 3
What does the 5 percent service fee include

Answer 3
The 5 percent service fee covers production management and QC coordination. It's calculated on FOB order value only. Packaging, labeling, kitting, logistics, customs, and duties are quoted separately as pass-through items billed at cost with zero markup.

Question 4
What does pass-through at cost mean

Answer 4
We bill you exactly what we pay for logistics, customs, duties, packaging, labeling, and kitting. Zero markup. You see every line item before you commit. This means you pay the same price we pay, with no hidden fees or markups.

Question 5
How long does it take to get factory quotes

Answer 5
After you deposit 49 dollars, you'll receive 3 verified factory quotes within 7 business days. This includes MOQ, lead time, and key compliance flags. FOB prices are locked for 3 months once quoted.

Question 6
What is excluded from the 5 percent fee

Answer 6
The 5 percent fee covers production management and QC coordination only. It does not include logistics, customs, duties, packaging, labeling, or kitting. These are all pass-through items billed at actual cost with zero markup.

Question 7
How accurate are the cost estimates

Answer 7
Our AI snapshot uses supplier quotes, reference transactions, and live duty and freight data. It's designed to be directionally accurate for budgeting and go or no-go decisions. Final pricing is confirmed when you receive factory quotes.

Question 8
What does Net 30 qualified mean

Answer 8
Net 30 payment terms allow you to pay invoices within 30 days of delivery. Qualification is based on credit history and order volume. Contact sales to learn more about qualification requirements.

Question 9
Can I use NexSupply if I already have a supplier

Answer 9
Yes. You can bring your own suppliers and use NexSupply for cost and risk checks, or to benchmark new options. We work with your existing partners when you need clearer numbers for internal decisions.

Question 10
What happens if I don't place an order

Answer 10
Your 49 dollar deposit is credited to your first invoice when you place an order. If you don't proceed with an order, deposit policies may vary by project. Contact support to discuss your specific situation. The free analysis has no commitment and no deposit required.
