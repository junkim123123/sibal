## FAQ SECTION (TOP OBJECTIONS)

**Section Title:** "Common questions"

**Q1: What is the $49 deposit for?**
The deposit reserves your dedicated sourcing expert and activates your project. It prioritizes factory quoting and ensures you receive 3 verified quotes within 7 business days. Your deposit is 100% credited to your first invoice when you place an order.

**Q2: Is the deposit refundable?**
Your deposit is applied as credit to your first invoice when you place an order. If you don't proceed with an order, deposit policies may vary by project. Contact our support team to discuss your specific situation.

**Q3: What does the 5% service fee include?**
The 5% service fee covers production management and QC coordination. It's calculated on FOB order value. Packaging, labeling, kitting, logistics, customs, and duties are quoted separately as pass-through items (billed at cost, zero markup).

**Q4: What does "pass-through at cost" mean?**
We bill you exactly what we pay for logistics, customs, duties, packaging, labeling, and kitting. Zero markup. You see every line item before you commit.

**Q5: How accurate are the cost estimates?**
Our AI snapshot uses supplier quotes, reference transactions, and live duty and freight data. It's designed to be directionally accurate for budgeting and go/no-go decisions. Final pricing is confirmed when you receive factory quotes.

**Q6: What if I already have a supplier?**
You can bring your own suppliers and use NexSupply for cost and risk checks, or to benchmark new options. We work with your existing partners when you need clearer numbers for internal decisions.

**Q7: How long does it take to get factory quotes?**
After you deposit $49, you'll receive 3 verified factory quotes within 7 business days. This includes MOQ, lead time, and key compliance flags.

**Q8: What does "Net-30 qualified" mean?**
Net-30 payment terms allow you to pay invoices within 30 days of delivery. Qualification is based on credit history and order volume. Contact sales to learn more.

## IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

### Tone Guidelines
- Direct and specific. No fluff.
- Trust-building through transparency, not hype.
- Use plain language a first-time importer understands.
- Short sentences. High clarity.

### Key Terms (Use Consistently)
- "Landed cost" (not "total cost" or "all-in cost")
- "Pass-through at cost" (not "at cost" or "pass-through")
- "Service fee" (not "management fee" or "execution fee")
- "Factory quotes" (not "supplier quotes" or "vendor quotes")
- "Deposit credited to first invoice" (not "refunded" or "applied")

### Risk Reversal Language (True & Compliant)
- "Deposit credited to first order" (not "refundable")
- "No credit card required for initial analysis"
- "See every line item before you commit"
- "Transparent pricing, no hidden fees"
- "Zero markup on pass-through costs"

### CTA Hierarchy
1. Primary: "Start free audit" (most prominent)
2. Secondary: "See how it works" or "See sample report" (text link or outline button)
3. Tertiary: "Contact sales" (for execution plan only)
